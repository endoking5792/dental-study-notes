### Introduction

Sonic and ultrasonic devices are adjunctive technologies that use oscillating instruments to improve mechanical cleaning, access and selected procedural tasks in endodontics. [SRC: Cohen's Pathways of the Pulp, p.285-286, "acoustic streaming and cavitation"] [Certain] Their value is greatest when they address areas that conventional rotary files cannot directly contact, but they should not be treated as substitutes for sound shaping, irrigation and clinical judgment. ### 1. Physical basis

Ultrasonic devices used for endodontic irrigation operate at frequencies around 25 to 30 kHz, whereas sonic devices operate at much lower frequencies. [SRC: Cohen's Pathways of the Pulp, p.285-286, "acoustic streaming and cavitation"] [Certain]

The oscillating instrument transfers mechanical energy to the irrigant, producing acoustic streaming and, under suitable conditions, cavitation. [SRC: Cohen's Pathways of the Pulp, p.285-286, "acoustic streaming and cavitation"] [Certain]

The clinical objective is increased fluid movement, improved debris displacement and better access of irrigant to recesses and anatomically complex areas. [SRC: Cohen's Pathways of the Pulp, p.285-286, "acoustic streaming and cavitation"] [Certain]

### 2. Sonic activation

Sonic systems use flexible noncutting polymer or similar tips to agitate the irrigant. [SRC: Cohen's Pathways of the Pulp, p.285-286, "acoustic streaming and cavitation"] [Certain]

Their lower frequency produces a different oscillation pattern from ultrasonic activation. [SRC: Cohen's Pathways of the Pulp, p.285-286, "acoustic streaming and cavitation"] [Certain]

They are useful as adjuncts for irrigant agitation and are generally less aggressive to dentin than actively cutting ultrasonic instrumentation. [RECALL] [Likely]

### 3. Ultrasonic irrigation

Two broad approaches are described:

A. **Ultrasonic instrumentation:** the file contacts the canal wall and simultaneously cuts and agitates irrigant. [SRC: Cohen's Pathways of the Pulp, p.285-286, "acoustic streaming and cavitation"] [Certain]

B. **Passive ultrasonic irrigation:** a small noncutting file or smooth wire is activated in irrigant after shaping. [SRC: Cohen's Pathways of the Pulp, p.285-286, "acoustic streaming and cavitation"] [Certain]

PUI is conceptually preferable when the objective is irrigant activation without additional intentional dentin cutting. [RECALL] [Likely]
