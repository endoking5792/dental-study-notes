## UNIT: RECENT ADVANCES

### Compiled from 3 source papers (TN MGR), 32 exam sittings, 1990–2016

This subject is structurally different from the other three: each question is worth the full 25 or 100 marks on its own (either a single either/or 100-mark essay in the 1990-2002 era, or four compulsory 25-mark essays in the 2007-2016 era). There is no short-note/short-essay tier here — every entry below is a full essay-length question. Because “Recent Advances” spans both Conservative Dentistry and Endodontics by definition, topics from both parent subjects appear side by side, exactly as the papers present them.

Source key: **(EWE 90-00)** = Essay With Emphasis on Recent Advances, 1990-2000 · **(EWE 01-02)** = Essay With Emphasis on Recent Advances, 2001-2002 · **(RACD 07-16)** = Recent Advances in Conservative Dentistry, 2007-2016

High-yield key: ★★★★★ 6+ sittings · ★★★★ 4-5 · ★★★ 3 · ★★ 2 · ★ 1 (scaled down from the other repositories since this batch has only 32 sittings total, each contributing 1-4 questions rather than 7-10)

## STRUCTURAL OBSERVATION (not a syllabus gap, but worth noting for exam strategy)

Because this paper format asks 1-4 full-essay questions per sitting rather than a large bank of short items, topic coverage across 32 sittings is inherently sparser per-topic than the other three repositories. A candidate preparing from this repository alone would see real topic diversity but low repetition — the practical implication is that “Recent Advances” preparation should draw on the SAME topics as they appear in the Conservative Dentistry and Endodontics repositories (cross-referenced throughout above), since a topic asked as a short note there (e.g., “Bioceramic sealers,” “MTA,” “Self-adjusting files”) may reappear here as a full 25-mark essay demanding much greater depth.
