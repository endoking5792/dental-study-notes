## EXAM MODE

**Definition.** Microleakage is the clinically undetectable passage of bacteria, fluids, molecules or ions along the interface between a restoration or root filling and the tooth (Sikri, 2020, p.435).

**Clinical significance in endodontics.**
1. Coronal leakage is proposed to contribute to treatment failure, based on in-vitro leakage studies (Berman et al, 2021, PDF p.1068).
2. Obturation reduces coronal leakage and bacterial contamination, seals the apex from periapical fluids, and entombs the remaining irritants (Berman et al, 2021, PDF p.1068).
3. Coronal microleakage can occur quickly through a canal that looks well obturated, risking periapical infection, so the coronal seal is now seen as important as the apical one (Berman et al, 2021, PDF p.1152).
4. Orifice barriers reduce coronal leakage in vitro (Chen et al, 2023). Their meta-analysis found no significant difference among GIC, RMGI, composite and MTA barriers. Mehta et al (2022) report MTA as least leaking, so the material ranking is contested [LIT, full text].

## HIGH-YIELD POINTS
- Microleakage = undetectable passage along the interface (Sikri, 2020, p.435).
- Coronal leakage can cause treatment failure (Berman et al, 2021, PDF p.1068).
- Obturation entombs irritants and reduces coronal leakage (Berman et al, 2021, PDF p.1068).
- Recontamination: after 19 days' exposure of the coronal filling, over 50% of gutta-percha and sealer-filled canals were fully contaminated (Torabinejad et al, 1990, PDF p.1) [LIT, full text].
- Clinical association, cross-sectional: good root fillings and good coronal restorations were each associated with periapical health. The combined good-good group had 91.4% absence of periapical inflammation, against 18.1% for poor-poor (Ray and Trope, 1995, PDF p.1) [LIT, full text]. Causality is not established.
- Sealer effect differs in vitro and in vivo: AH26 leaked most in vitro (Madison et al, 1987), but sealers did not differ in vivo (Madison and Wilcox, 1988). See Q8.

## COMMON EXAM MISTAKES

2. Stating the coronal-failure link as proven. The source calls it proposed, based on in-vitro studies.
